Home FieldNFL +1.75 points; college +2.5 points; 0 for neutral-site games
ProbabilityPredicted margin converted through logistic probability
MarketOptional 40% market / 60% independent model blend, averaged (no-vig) across every US book The Odds API returns
AtsAgainst-the-spread mode ranks and picks on cover probability: the model's margin is blended 40% toward the market's implied margin (mirroring the moneyline market blend) before comparing to the consensus sportsbook spread, instead of straight-up win probability. Games without a quoted spread fall back to straight-up for that game and are flagged with ats_warning.
Field ConsensusStraight-up mode blends 15% toward the field's own pick percentage. ATS mode fades it instead - heavy public backing on a side is treated as a mild signal against that side of the number.
LeverageModel win probability minus field ownership
ConfidenceGames ranked by decision score and assigned descending points
