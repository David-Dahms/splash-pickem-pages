Methodology

Historical DataPrevious-season completed ESPN games
Team Rating40% scoring margin, 30% win percentage, 30% offensive production
Home FieldNFL +1.75 points; college +2.5 points
ProbabilityPredicted margin converted through logistic probability
MarketOptional 40% market / 60% independent model blend, averaged (no-vig) across every US book The Odds API returns
AtsAgainst-the-spread mode ranks and picks using model margin vs. the consensus sportsbook spread, instead of straight-up win probability. Games without a quoted spread fall back to straight-up for that game and are flagged with ats_warning.
LeverageModel win probability minus field ownership
ConfidenceGames ranked by decision score and assigned descending points
NoteResearch/decision-support model; not a guarantee of outcomes.
Model %/ATS %/spreads are research/decision-support estimates, not a guarantee of outcomes.
